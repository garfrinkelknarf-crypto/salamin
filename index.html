Salamin

Classroom video reflection

For your own reflection only. Salamin gives you a private draft about your own lesson. It is not an evaluation. Never use it for IPCRF, COT, ranking or any decision about a teacher.

How will you use this?

Classroom video

Required. Pick the recording from this phone or laptop. The file stays on this device. Most phone videos work (MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, 3GP). Old formats such as AVI, WMV or FLV usually cannot play in a browser.

Transcript of the class talk

Optional, but needed for feedback on questioning, who talks, and how Filipino and English are used. Still frames cannot show these.

Make it here

Salamin listens to your video and writes down what was said, with times. It runs on this phone or laptop, free, with no account, and the sound is not uploaded. The first time, it downloads a speech model (about 80 to 300 MB) and keeps it for next time. A laptop with Chrome or Edge is much faster than a phone. On a phone, you can do only part of the video, and you can stop at any time and keep what is done.

Only part of the video (faster on a phone)

Write times like 12:30. If the transcript covers less than half of the video, results that depend on it show at most Some evidence.

Or add one you already have:

The downloaded Salamin app (Salamin.html or the installed app) can make the transcript for you. Here inside Claude, paste one or choose a file.

You can paste text or choose a .txt, .srt, .vtt or .docx file. Any language mix is fine. If you use another transcription app, learners' voices go to that service. Check that your school allows it.

                          How your data is handled

                          Always stays on this device

                          • Your video file. It plays from the file you picked and is never uploaded.
                          • Audio, the file name, and any blur boxes you draw.
                          • Making a transcript here also runs on this device. The sound is not uploaded. Only the speech engine and model are downloaded, once, from cdn.jsdelivr.net and huggingface.co.
                          • No account, no server, no analytics.

                          Leaves this device only when you tap "Get feedback"

                          • The few low-resolution preview images you checked, plus the lesson plan, transcript and criteria text you see in the "See exactly what text is sent" box. Names you listed are replaced with [name] first.
                          • Inside Claude, they go to Claude on your own Claude account to write the feedback. Claude asks your permission first and it uses a little of your usage. Your Claude privacy settings apply.
                          • In a downloaded copy you choose. Either you upload the blurred images and paste the prompt into Claude chat yourself, or your browser sends them straight to Anthropic with your own API key. Salamin has no server in between. Check Anthropic's current data terms for the API and your school's data rules.
                          • An API key you type is kept in the open tab only and is never saved.
                          • Nothing is sent if you tap "Skip AI and just reflect".

                          Built-in safeguards

                          • Before anything is sent to AI you must preview the exact images and confirm no learner can be recognized. This is the same in both modes.
                          • Quick feedback has no checklist and its reports are stamped as personal drafts. Formal report needs the consent checklist, and the report records it.
                          • Strict mode keeps very little detail, but a face close to the camera can still be recognized by someone who knows the learner. Boxes cover faces completely.
                          • Claude is told not to identify anyone, not to read emotions, and not to describe anyone's appearance or personal traits.
                          • The feedback is a draft. You can discard any line. Ratings are your own.
                          • For growth, not ranking. No school-wide totals, no leaderboards.

                          Follow your school's rules

                          Check these with your school head or Data Protection Officer: the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), DepEd's Child Protection Policy, and DepEd Order No. 003, s. 2026 on AI in basic education. That order asks schools to do a Privacy Impact Assessment before they deploy an AI system, to list it in the DepEd AI Registry, to disclose AI use, and to keep people in charge of decisions. It bans facial recognition and emotion recognition. Salamin does neither. This is not legal advice.

                          Draft on this browser

                          Off by default. If you turn it on, your lesson details, plan text, ratings, notes, marked moments, the name and school for formal reports, and the AI feedback with transcript quotes removed (never the video, images, transcript or names list) are kept in this browser until you erase them. Do not turn it on if other people use this browser.

                          What Salamin looks at

                          Made for: private reflection on your own lesson.

                          Never use it for: IPCRF, COT or any rating, ranking, discipline, judging another teacher's lesson, or any decision about a person.

                          Eight criteria. Each one shows Evidence found, Some evidence, Not enough evidence, or Runs disagreed, with the evidence. There are no AI scores. Your own self rating stays yours.

                          What the software checks (not the AI)

                          • Every quote and time Claude cites must really exist in your transcript. Comments whose evidence cannot be found are removed, and one citation that cannot be found lowers Evidence found to Some evidence.
                          • Talk, questioning, language, accuracy and checking are locked to Not enough evidence when there is no transcript. Talk also needs speaker labels. If most labels are Salamin's guesses that you did not confirm, talk shows at most Some evidence.
                          • With No images (text only), what learners are doing and safety are locked to Not enough evidence, and lesson structure shows at most Some evidence.
                          • If Salamin made the transcript and you did not review it, transcript-based results show at most Some evidence.
                          • Salamin asks Claude twice. If the two checks differ, the lower result is shown. If they are far apart it says Runs disagreed. Comments only one check made are removed.

                          What nobody has checked yet

                          • These criteria are a draft. They are not validated and not an official observation tool.
                          • How closely Claude agrees with trained human observers has not been measured. Your Agree and Disagree marks help, and the pilot table in step 3 lets a school measure it properly.
                          • How well speech recognition handles Taglish has not been measured. When you review a transcript Salamin made, the report shows how many words you changed: a rough measure for your class.

                          What it cannot do

                          • It sees at most 12 blurred frames, not the whole lesson, and hears no audio, tone or silence. With No images (text only), it sees nothing and reads only your text.
                          • It cannot check that Claude read the images or the Filipino correctly. Only you can.
                          • It cannot judge safety, individual learners, or the quality of a teacher as a person.
                          • Mixing Filipino and English is treated as a learning resource, never as an error.
